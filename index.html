About

Sweet Crumbs started in a small home kitchen with a big love for baking. Every cupcake, cookie and loaf is made by hand in small batches, using simple, good ingredients.

We bake to order, so everything reaches you fresh. Whether it's a birthday, a school event or just a Tuesday treat, we'd love to bake something special for you.
